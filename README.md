# Marg Analytics — Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify revenue trends, product performance, regional contribution, customer purchasing behavior, and sales channel performance.

The workbook includes source sales data, Pivot Tables, and a sales dashboard to support data-driven business decisions.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance and average order value.
- Identify top-performing product categories and products.
- Compare sales performance across regions and channels.
- Evaluate revenue contribution from new and returning customers.
- Examine monthly sales trends and salesperson performance.
- Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- KPI Analysis
- Data Analysis and Visualization
- Business Intelligence and Reporting

## Key Business Insights

1. **Category performance:** Computers contribute approximately 54.9% of total revenue, making them the leading product category.
2. **Sales channels:** Online is the highest-revenue channel, followed by Corporate Sales and Retail Stores.
3. **Customer behavior:** Returning customers contribute approximately 68.7% of revenue.
4. **Product concentration:** The five highest-revenue products contribute approximately 57.7% of total revenue.
5. **Regional performance:** North and Central are the two leading regions by revenue, with closely matched results.
6. **Monthly trends:** Revenue varies considerably across the year, highlighting the importance of monthly performance monitoring.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory planning for high-revenue products.
- Investigate opportunities to improve marketplace performance.
- Strengthen customer retention and repeat-purchase initiatives.
- Compare regional performance by product mix and sales channel.
- Evaluate discount effectiveness using revenue and profit-margin data.

## Data Quality Considerations

- The workbook contains 2,000 unique order IDs.
- The source dataset includes records dated through December 2026. Future-dated records should be validated before presenting the results as actual sales performance.
- Existing Pivot Tables and dashboard figures should be reconciled against the full source dataset before final reporting.
